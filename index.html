End-to-End Workflow

From raw laser returns to actionable field orders.

A controlled, 6-stage engineering process ensuring maximum precision.

01 / SCOPE

Corridor Scoping

Define corridor extents, clearance standards, and coordinate system requirements.

02 / CAPTURE

Data Acquisition

High-density LiDAR and multispectral capture via UAV, aerial, or mobile platforms.

03 / CLASSIFY

Point-Cloud Class

Segment ground, canopy, towers, and conductors using automated AI pipelines.

04 / ANALYZE

Vegetation Risk

Compute 3D proximity, growth buffers, and hazard tree fall-in geometry.

05 / VALIDATE

QA & Verification

Review danger-zone flags with utility foresters for prioritised field execution.

06 / DELIVER

Handoff & Export

Export directly to Enterprise GIS, work order management tools, and CAD/BIM.

Flagship Capabilities

Vegetation classification parameters

Configurable parameters driving automated pruning work orders.

Danger-zone classification inputs

Clearance Distance Line-Voltage Specific

Minimum 3D buffer envelope from conductor to surrounding canopy returns.

Growth-Rate Buffer Species-Informed

Predictive growth modeled across seasonal inspection intervals.

Species Risk Class Growth & Brittleness

Weighted scoring based on structural failure and growth aggressiveness.

Fall-Zone Radius Height-to-Line Arc

Identifies tall off-corridor hazard trees that can strike conductors if felled.

* Thresholds are customized per client utility standards (e.g. NERC FAC-003, IEEE, or regional forestry guidelines).
