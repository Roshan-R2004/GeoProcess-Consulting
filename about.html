End-to-end workflow

Six stages. One controlled pipeline.

01 / SCOPE

Corridor Scoping

Define corridor extents, clearance standards and coordinate-system requirements.

02 / CAPTURE

Data Acquisition

High-density LiDAR and multispectral capture via UAV, aerial or mobile platforms.

03 / CLASSIFY

Point-Cloud Class

Segment ground, canopy, towers and conductors using automated pipelines.

04 / ANALYZE

Risk & Surface Modeling

Compute proximity, growth buffers, hazard fall arcs and bare-earth DTM grids.

05 / VALIDATE

QA & Verification

Review danger-zone flags and elevation surfaces with specialists.

06 / DELIVER

Handoff & Export

Export to enterprise GIS, work-order tools and CAD/BIM workflows.
